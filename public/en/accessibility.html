Our starting point

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including with a keyboard, a screen reader, magnification or a slow connection. We aim to meet the WCAG 2.2 guidelines at level AA.

What we have done

  • All features work with the keyboard, with a clearly visible focus.
  • A logical heading structure, recognisable landmarks and a link to skip straight to the content.
  • Descriptive alternative text for every portfolio photo.
  • Sufficient colour contrast and text that scales with your browser settings.
  • Form fields with labels and error messages that are announced by assistive technology.
  • If your operating system asks for reduced motion, animations are switched off and you see the calm final state straight away.
  • Lightweight pages without trackers, so the site loads quickly even on a slow connection.

Known limitations

The Google reviews are shown in the original language (English) in which clients wrote them. They are marked as such so that screen readers use the right pronunciation.

Found a problem?
