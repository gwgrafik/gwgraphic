03 / Process

From idea to finished result.

First I find out what you need. Then the project goes into production and, where required, installation.

  1. 01

    Design

    Together we agree on the scope, style and use. I then develop the design and refine it before it goes into production.

  2. 02

    Production

    Once approved, the materials are prepared for the right production method: print, vinyl, garment printing or online publication.

  3. 03

    Installation

    Vehicle, shop window and glass graphics are installed on location. Everything else is delivered ready to use.
