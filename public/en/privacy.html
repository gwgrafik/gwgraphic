Who is responsible?

GW Graphic Design, the studio of Grzegorz Woźniak (North Brabant, the Netherlands), is the controller for the processing of personal data described in this privacy policy.

Questions about privacy or a request about your data? Email design@gwgraphic.com or call +31 6 44 31 94 15.

What data do we process?

  • Contact details you give us: name, company name, email address and phone number.
  • Project information you send us: your message, the services you choose and any files you send by email or WhatsApp.
  • Client and order data: quotes, agreements, invoices and payments.
  • Technical data: when you submit the form, your IP address is hashed (made unrecognisable) and kept for no more than one hour to prevent abuse. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server log files (such as IP address, time and requested page) for security.

We do not ask for special categories of personal data and do not need them.

The quote form

What you enter in the form is sent over a secure connection (HTTPS) to our server, checked there and delivered as an email to design@gwgraphic.com. We only use this data to answer your request and, if you place an order, to carry out the project. The form contains no trackers and no files are uploaded.

Who do we share data with?

We never sell data and only share it when needed for the purpose:

  • our hosting and email provider, which runs the website and mailbox;
  • our accountant or accounting software for invoices and bookkeeping;
  • production partners or carriers, only when your order requires it (for example a delivery address).

We make agreements on security and confidentiality with processors. If you contact us via WhatsApp, WhatsApp (Meta) processes your data under its own terms and privacy policy. Viewing this website does not activate WhatsApp.

Transfers outside the EEA

Where possible we choose service providers that process data within the European Economic Area. If a provider processes data outside the EEA, this only happens with an appropriate safeguard, such as an adequacy decision or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long do we keep data?

  • Requests that do not lead to an order: no more than 12 months after the last contact.
  • Client and project data: for as long as we work together and afterwards for as long as needed for warranty or questions about the delivered work.
  • Invoices and accounting records: 7 years, as required by Dutch tax law.
  • Hashed IP addresses for abuse prevention: no more than 1 hour.

Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures: an encrypted connection (HTTPS), secure server settings, limited access to data, backups and regular updates. The form is protected against spam and abuse without third-party tracking or CAPTCHA.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability. If you have given consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before. Send your request to design@gwgraphic.com. We respond within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity.

Making a complaint

Not happy with how we handle your data? Please tell us first so we can find a solution together. You always have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Cookies and external services

This website does not use cookies for statistics, advertising or tracking and does not load third-party fonts, scripts or widgets. Read more in the cookie policy. Links to Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn only open an external service when you click them.

Changes

We update this privacy policy when our way of working, service providers or the law change. The date of the latest change is shown at the top of this page.
