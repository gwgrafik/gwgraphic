Who is responsible?

GW Graphic Design, a sole proprietorship operated by Grzegorz Woźniak in North Brabant, the Netherlands, is the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Questions about privacy or a request about your data? Email design@gwgraphic.com or call +31 6 44 31 94 15.

What data do I process?

  • Contact details you provide to me: name, company name, email address and phone number.
  • Project information you send to me: your message, the services you select and any files you send by email or WhatsApp.
  • Client and order data: quotes, agreements, invoices and payments.
  • Technical data: when you submit the form, your IP address is hashed (made unrecognisable) and kept for no more than one hour to prevent abuse. The hosting provider also keeps standard server log files (such as IP address, time and requested page) for security purposes.

I do not ask for special categories of personal data and they are not needed to handle your enquiry or order.

The quote form

What you enter in the form is sent over a secure connection (HTTPS) to the server and delivered as an email to design@gwgraphic.com. I only use this information to reply to your enquiry and, if you place an order, to carry it out. The form contains no trackers and cannot be used to upload files.

Who do I share data with?

I do not sell personal data. I only share it where and to the extent necessary for a specific purpose:

  • the hosting and email provider that runs the website and mailbox;
  • an accountant or accounting software for invoices and bookkeeping;
  • production partners or carriers, only when your order requires it (for example a delivery address).

I make arrangements on security and confidentiality with anyone who processes data on my behalf. If you contact me via WhatsApp, WhatsApp (Meta) processes your data under its own terms and privacy policy. Simply viewing this website does not activate WhatsApp.

Transfers outside the EEA

Where possible, I choose service providers that process data within the European Economic Area. If a provider processes data outside the EEA, this only happens with an appropriate safeguard, such as an adequacy decision or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

How long do I keep data?

  • Requests that do not lead to an order: no more than 12 months after the last contact.
  • Client and project data: for as long as we work together, and afterwards for as long as needed for warranty matters or questions about the delivered work.
  • Invoices and accounting records: 7 years, as required by Dutch tax law.
  • Hashed IP addresses for abuse prevention: no more than 1 hour.

Security

I take appropriate technical and organisational measures: an encrypted connection (HTTPS), secure server settings, limited access to data, backups and regular updates. The form is protected against spam and abuse without third-party tracking or CAPTCHA.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and data portability. If you have given consent, you can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect processing that took place before. Send your request to design@gwgraphic.com. I respond within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity.

Making a complaint

If you have concerns about how your data is handled, please contact me first. I will try to clarify the issue and find a solution. You always have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Cookies and external services

This website does not use cookies for analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not load third-party fonts, scripts or widgets. Read more in the cookie policy. Links to Google, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn only open an external service when you click them.

Changes

I update this privacy policy when the way the website works, the service providers used or the law change. The date of the latest change is shown at the top of this page.
