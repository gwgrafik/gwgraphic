Scope

These terms apply to all quotes, orders and deliveries provided by GW Graphic Design (Grzegorz Woźniak), unless a quote or written agreement states otherwise. They cover, among other things, graphic design, logos and visual identity, print, branded clothing, signage, vehicle graphics, window graphics, websites, promotional products and installation.

Quotes and orders

A quote is based on the information available when it is prepared and is valid for 30 days unless stated otherwise.

An order is accepted once you approve the quote in writing, including by email or WhatsApp.

I discuss any changes or work outside the agreed scope before carrying them out, and additional work may be quoted separately.

Deposit and payment

Once the scope of the order has been agreed, I require a deposit of at least 50% of the total order value unless the quote states otherwise.

The remaining balance is due after the design has been approved and before printing or production begins.

If an order does not include printing or production, the due date for the remaining balance is stated in the quote or invoice.

Invoices must be paid by the due date shown on the invoice. I may pause production, delivery of final files or installation while a due payment remains outstanding.

Materials you provide

You are responsible for the accuracy of any text, names, contact details, dimensions and other materials you provide, and you confirm that you have the right to use them.

I do not accept work containing unlawful content or material that infringes the rights of others.

Proof and approval

Before a design goes into production, you will receive a version to review.

Please check spelling, numbers, colours, dimensions and placement carefully.

Once approved, the design goes into production.

If an error was visible in the approved design, responsibility for approving it rests with the client. If an error is introduced on my side during production or execution, I will correct it.

Revisions

The number of design proposals and revision rounds included in the price is stated in the quote.

Additional revision rounds, a change of direction or new elements are discussed before the work is carried out and may be quoted separately.

Colour, materials and tolerances

Colours displayed on a screen may differ from colours on paper, fabric or vinyl.

Small variations in colour, cutting, placement, print, material batch or dimensions that fall within normal production tolerances are not considered defects.

If a particular material has relevant limitations, I will explain them before production.

Vehicle graphics and installation

Before installation, tell me about any damage, resprayed panels, coatings, previous repairs or other relevant characteristics of the surface.

Adhesion and later removal of vinyl depend on factors including the condition and age of the paint or glass, previous repairs, temperature and the type of surface.

The vehicle or window must be clean and accessible at the agreed time. I will discuss any specific risks with you before installation.

Websites

A website includes the functions and content stated in the quote.

Hosting, domain registration, paid plugins, third-party licences, maintenance, copywriting and updates are included only when explicitly agreed.

After handover, you are responsible for updates, backups and security unless a separate maintenance agreement states otherwise.

Intellectual property

Sketches, unused concepts and working files remain the property of GW Graphic Design unless agreed otherwise.

Once full payment has been received, you receive the right to use the final design within the scope described in the quote.

Fonts, images, plugins, software and other third-party materials remain subject to their own licences.

Portfolio

Unless I have agreed confidentiality with you in advance, I may show completed work that is publicly visible in the GW Graphic Design portfolio and on social media.

If you do not want a particular project to be published, please let me know in writing before publication.

Delivery times

Stated delivery times are estimates and depend on factors including timely approval, payment, material availability and production by third parties.

If a delay occurs for reasons outside my control, I will let you know about the revised timing as soon as possible.

Cancellation

If you cancel an order after work has started, you are responsible for the work already completed and any costs that have already been irrevocably incurred with third parties.

Consumers retain their statutory rights. Custom-made or personalised products may be excluded from the right of withdrawal where permitted by law.

Complaints

Please report visible defects or missing items as soon as reasonably possible after delivery, preferably with photos.

I will review the issue first and propose an appropriate solution.

This does not limit any statutory rights available to you.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, GW Graphic Design is not liable for indirect loss or loss of profit, and liability is limited to the invoice amount for the order concerned.

This limitation does not apply in cases of intent, gross negligence or where liability cannot legally be limited.

Consumers

If you are a consumer, mandatory consumer protection law takes precedence over any provision of these terms that conflicts with it.

Governing law

Dutch law applies to all orders.

If a dispute arises, I will first try to resolve it with you amicably before the matter is referred to the competent court.
