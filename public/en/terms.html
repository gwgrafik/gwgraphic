Scope

These terms apply to all quotes, orders and deliveries by GW Graphic Design (Grzegorz Woźniak) unless the quote or a written agreement states otherwise. They cover, among other things, graphic design, logo and visual identity, print, printed apparel, signs and signage, vehicle lettering and graphics, window graphics, websites, promotional products and installation.

Quotes and orders

A quote is based on the information known at the time and is valid for 30 days unless stated otherwise. An order is placed as soon as you accept the quote in writing (including by email or WhatsApp). Changes or extra work outside the agreed scope are discussed in advance and may be quoted separately.

Deposit and payment

A deposit, usually 50%, may be requested before a project starts. The balance and any instalments are stated in the quote or on the invoice. Invoices are paid within the term stated on the invoice. While a due payment is outstanding, production, delivery of final files or installation may be postponed.

Materials you supply

You are responsible for the accuracy of text, names, contact details, dimensions and other material you supply, and you confirm that you have the right to have this material used. We do not carry out orders with unlawful or infringing content.

Proof and approval

Before anything goes into production, you receive a design or proof to check. Please check spelling, numbers, colours, dimensions and placement carefully. After your approval the work goes into production. Errors found after approval that were in the approved proof are at your expense; errors in our own execution we put right.

Revisions

The number of included design proposals and revision rounds is stated in the quote. Extra rounds, a new direction or additional items are discussed in advance.

Colour, materials and tolerances

Colours on a screen may differ from colours on paper, textiles or vinyl. Small deviations in colour, cutting, placement, printing, material batches or dimensions within normal industry standards are not a defect. Limitations of a specific material are discussed in advance.

Lettering and installation

Before installation, tell us about damage, resprayed parts, coatings, repairs or other particularities of the surface. Adhesion and later removal depend on the condition and age of the paint or glass, previous repairs, temperature and the surface. The vehicle or window must be clean and accessible at the agreed time. Specific risks are recorded with you before installation.

Websites

A website includes the features and content stated in the quote. Hosting, domain name, paid plugins, third-party licences, maintenance, copywriting and updates are only included when explicitly agreed. After delivery you are responsible for updates, backups and security unless a maintenance agreement says otherwise.

Intellectual property

Sketches, unchosen proposals and working files remain the property of GW Graphic Design unless agreed otherwise. After full payment you receive the usage rights to the final design as described in the quote. Third-party fonts, images, plugins and software are subject to their own licences.

Portfolio

Unless confidentiality was agreed in advance, GW Graphic Design may show delivered work that is publicly visible in its portfolio and on social media. If you do not want this, please tell us in writing before publication.

Delivery and planning

Stated lead times are estimates and depend on timely feedback, payment, material availability and production by third parties. If a delay beyond our control occurs, we will inform you of a new schedule as soon as possible.

Cancellation

If you cancel an order after work has started, you pay for the work already done and the costs already irrevocably incurred with third parties. Consumers keep their statutory rights; custom-made and personalised products are legally exempt from the right of withdrawal.

Complaints

Report visible defects or missing items as soon as possible after delivery, preferably with photos. We will work with you to find a suitable solution. This does not affect your statutory rights.

Liability

To the extent permitted by law, GW Graphic Design is not liable for indirect or consequential damage, and liability is limited to the invoice amount of the order concerned. This limitation does not apply in case of intent or deliberate recklessness, or where the law does not allow a limitation.

Consumers

If you are a consumer, mandatory consumer law takes precedence over any conflicting provision in these terms.

Governing law

Dutch law applies to all orders. We first try to resolve disputes together before either party goes to the competent court.
