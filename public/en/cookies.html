In short

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising networks, social media pixels or other trackers. Fonts, images and scripts are all loaded from our own server. That is also why there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to ask consent for.

What is stored?

NameTypePurposeRetention
gwIntroVisitslocalStorageCounts your visits (one per browser session), so that from the fourth visit you get the option to switch the opening animation off.Until you clear it via Cookie settings or your browser
gwIntroDisabledlocalStorageOnly if you choose ‘Don’t show again’ yourself: remembers that choice.Until you clear it or switch the animation back on
gwIntroSessionsessionStoragePrevents the animation from starting again during the same visit.Until you close the tab

These are functional preferences: they stay on your own device, are never sent to us, are not used for statistics or advertising and cannot identify you.

Server log files

Like every website, our hosting provider records technical data about requests to the server (such as IP address, time and requested page) to keep the website secure and available. This happens without cookies. Read more in the privacy policy.

External links

Links to Google Reviews, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn only open that service when you click them. From then on, that service’s cookie and privacy rules apply.

Managing your choice

Use Cookie settings at the bottom of every page to see this overview and clear stored data straight away. You can also delete storage at any time in your browser settings.

Changes

If we ever start using statistics or other non-essential technologies, we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent beforehand, with an equally easy option to refuse.
